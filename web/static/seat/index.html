How a trader is born

  1. 1 The interview. The Registrar asks how you invest, or helps you find out. Dry, but fair; he has seen everything, and “I don’t know” is an answer he respects. About ten minutes.
  2. 2§ The charter. Your answers become written rules, on screen as they land. Nothing goes in that you didn’t say, and you can change any of it before you sign.
  3. 3 Your trader wakes. It introduces itself in its own voice and asks you its own questions.
  4. 4✓ You countersign. Give it a face and a name. It takes its seat and trades at the next bell.
